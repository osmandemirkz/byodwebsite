What you touch

Three textures, nothing else

The whole game fits on one screen. No menus to learn, no progression to keep up with — just three materials that respond the way you expect them to.

Cold blocks

Firm, straight from the fridge. Tap and they split along a clean line; keep going and they break down into smaller pieces.

The warm pool

Drop a piece in and it softens, spreads and settles. Nothing is lost — it just changes state.

Crumble

What's left over gathers as loose crumb. Rake it with a finger, push it around, flatten it out again.

How to play

There isn't much to learn

01

Tap a cold block

One tap cracks it. The sound you hear is built on the spot from the way you touched it, so no two cracks land the same.

02

Drop a piece into the warm pool

Drag a piece over and let go. It melts at its own pace — there is nothing to wait for and nothing to collect.

03

Rake the crumble

Move a finger through what's left. The crumb shifts under it, piles up at the edges and evens out again.

04

Stop whenever you like

There is no session to finish and no streak to protect. Close the app mid-crack and nothing is lost.

Sound

Nothing here is a recording

Every crack, drop and rustle is generated live while you play, shaped by where and how hard you touched. That is why the game is 313 KB — there is no audio library inside it, only the instructions for making the sound. Headphones are worth it, but silent mode works too; the game is just as good without a sound.

Privacy

The game collects nothing

Butter asks for no account, shows no ads and sends nothing anywhere. It runs entirely on your device, online or off. Last updated: 22 September 2026.

What data does the app collect?

None. There is no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. Apple's App Store privacy label for Butter reads "Data Not Collected", and that is the whole story.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Everything happens on the device, so the game works on a plane, underground or with the connection switched off. It makes no network requests while you play.

Are there ads or purchases?

Neither. The game is free, complete, and there is nothing to buy inside it.

Is it suitable for children?

The App Store age rating is 4+. There is no text to read, no chat, no links out of the game and nothing to spend money on.

How do I remove my data?

Deleting the app removes everything, because everything it keeps is already on your device — a handful of settings and nothing more.
