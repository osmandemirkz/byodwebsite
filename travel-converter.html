What it does

Stop doing currency maths in your head

The price on the tag, the taxi fare, the hotel bill — instead of working out the exchange rate yourself, you type the amount and read it straight off in the currency you actually think in.

MULTI-CONVERT

Eight currencies at once

The list starts ready to go: dollar, euro, pound, yen, yuan and more. Build your own set from 157 currencies.

LIVE RATES

Refreshed every five minutes

Rates update automatically in the background, and the time of the last update is shown at the top of the screen. Pull fresh ones yourself any time with the refresh button.

OFFLINE

Keeps working without a signal

The latest rates are stored on the device. On a plane, in a country with no roaming, or anywhere with no reception, you can keep converting.

How to use it

All of it in six steps

01

Type the amount

Enter the amount on the keypad for the active currency at the top of the screen. Every other currency below recalculates with each digit you type.

02

Tap to switch direction

Tap any currency in the list and it becomes the active one — from then on, what you type is read as that currency. There is no separate swap button.

03

Build your own list

Add a currency with + — search by code (TRY) or by name and pick it. Remove one you don't need with the cross on its card.

04

Reorder and choose a view

Press and hold a card, then move it where you want it, so the currency you use most sits at the top. The icon in the top right switches between card view and list view.

05

Open the calculator when you need it

A four-function calculator is built in. Split a bill three ways or multiply by quantity, then convert the result straight away — without leaving the app.

06

Refresh the rates

Opening the app after a while away, tap the refresh icon. The "Last updated" line at the top always tells you when the rates were fetched.

FAQ

Common questions

How often are the rates updated?

While the app is open, rates refresh automatically every five minutes. You can also pull them in at any moment with the refresh icon in the top right. The time the rates were last fetched is shown at the top of the screen.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes. The most recent rates are stored on your device and used for conversions while you are offline. As soon as the connection is back, rates refresh on their own.

Which currencies are included?

All 157 currencies are in the list — alongside the usual dollar, euro, pound, yen and yuan you will find the Turkish lira, Uzbek som, Kazakhstani tenge, Russian ruble, and Gulf currencies such as the dirham and riyal. Bitcoin can be added to the list just like any other currency.

Do I have to set up my list every time?

No. The currencies you add and the order you put them in stay on your device, so the app opens with the same layout next time.

Are there ads, and do I need an account?

The app is ad-supported; ads are served through Google AdMob, and on iOS you are asked for permission before any personalised advertising. You don't need an account, an email address or a phone number to use it.

What happens to my data?

The app asks for no sign-up and collects no personal details. Your chosen currencies and the latest rates are stored only on your device; rates themselves are fetched from the app's rate server. See the privacy policy for the full detail.

Privacy

Travel Converter asks for no account and collects no personal details. Your currency list, your chosen order and the latest exchange rates are stored on your device only — they are never uploaded to us. Last updated: 21 September 2026.

ON DEVICE

The currencies you add, the order you arrange them in and the last rates fetched are saved on the device itself. Deleting the app removes them.

ADVERTISING

Ads through Google AdMob

The app is ad-supported. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may use an advertising identifier — on iOS only after you allow tracking.

What information does the app collect?

No personal information. The app has no sign-up, no contact form and no analytics account tied to you. The only data leaving your device is the request for current exchange rates, and the information an advertising provider needs to serve an ad.

Where do the exchange rates come from?

The app fetches rates from its rate server. That request carries only what any web request carries — it does not include your currency list, your amounts or anything that identifies you personally.

How does advertising work?

Ads are delivered by Google AdMob. For personalised advertising AdMob may use the device advertising identifier. On iOS the app asks for your permission first through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; if you decline, you still see ads, but non-personalised ones. Google's own privacy policy covers how it handles that data.

Is the app suitable for children?

The App Store age rating is 4+. The app collects no personal data from anyone, children included, and asks for no account.

How do I remove my data?

Everything the app stores lives on your device, so deleting the app deletes all of it. There is nothing held on our side to request or erase.
